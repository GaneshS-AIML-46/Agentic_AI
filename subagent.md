# Frontend Sections
The OptiChain frontend UI is composed of the following main sections:

1. **Form/Search Area**: A text area to input natural-language supply-chain problems to query the AI.
2. **KPIs**: Displays high-level metrics (Forecast, Total Cost, Delivery Time, Risk Score, and Solver Status).
3. **Supplier Allocation**: A table detailing how units are distributed across different suppliers and the associated costs.
4. **Routes**: A table showing the specific transportation lanes, modes (e.g., truck, rail), and quantities being shipped.
5. **Inventory Plan**: A table breaking down inventory by Distribution Center (DC), showing on-hand, reserved, available, and safety stock.
6. **What-If Scenarios**: A tool to rerun the solver under different stress conditions (Demand Increase, Supplier Failure, Price/Fuel Increase, Route Disruption, Inventory Shortage).
7. **Explainable Recommendation**: A textual explanation generated by the AI detailing how it derived the current plan based on the data.
8. **Retrieved Evidence**: Excerpts from documents (like playbooks) retrieved via RAG that influenced the decision.
9. **Agent Trace**: A step-by-step log showing the execution path of the LangGraph agents and any validation issues found.

# Backend Subagents
The backend utilizes a multi-agent workflow orchestrated by LangGraph. The specialized subagents are:

- **Demand Agent**: Analyzes historical data and generates demand forecasts for the requested products. It calculates how many units actually need to be fulfilled.
- **Supplier Agent**: Evaluates available suppliers based on capacity, pricing, reliability score, and risk tier. It filters out ineligible or failed suppliers (especially during "What-If" scenarios).
- **Route Agent**: Determines available transportation routes between the chosen suppliers and the destination warehouses, taking into account distance, cost, mode of transport, and any active disruptions.
- **Inventory Agent**: Checks current warehouse stock levels (on-hand, reserved, safety stock) to determine the net procurement required to satisfy the demand.
- **Risk Agent**: Evaluates risk events (like weather, congestion, fuel spikes) and calculates a composite risk score for the selected suppliers and routes.
